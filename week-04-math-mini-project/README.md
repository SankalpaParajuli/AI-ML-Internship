- Intuitive (not just formula-based) understanding of vectors, matrices, dot products, and matrix multiplication.
- Probability basics: conditional probability and common distributions (Normal, Binomial).
- Derivatives/gradients intuitively, and why gradient descent is the mechanism used to "train" models.
- Full independent EDA workflow on a new dataset: cleaning, exploration, visualization, and written insights.
